Window tint in Cedar City, for pretty much anything with glass.

Night Shift Tint tints cars, trucks, side-by-sides, heavy equipment, campers, homes and storefronts across Southern Utah. Bring it to the shop on 5400 West, or Tayson brings the film to you. Every vehicle gets Geoshield C2 ceramic film, cut on a plotter to fit your glass.

  1. Cars, trucks and SUVs

    Geoshield C2 ceramic film on daily drivers, work trucks and the one you only take out on Sundays. Ceramic keeps the cab cooler in a Cedar City July without having to go limo dark, and it won't mess with your phone or GPS.

  2. UTVs and side-by-sides

    RZR, Ranger, General, Xpedition, Maverick, Defender. A tinted cab is a lot easier to sit in after a day on the trails out of Brian Head or Three Peaks, and the plastics stop baking.

  3. Heavy equipment

    Excavators, dozers, skid steers, tractors. Operators sit in that glass box ten hours a day. Tint cuts the glare off the dirt and takes load off the cab A/C. We come to the job site or the yard.

  4. Campers and RVs

    Trailer and motorhome windows get the same treatment as your truck. More privacy at the campground and a cooler camper when it's parked in the sun.

  5. Home window tint

    Film for west-facing rooms and big picture windows. You keep the view, lose most of the glare, and your floors and couch stop fading. Films block up to 99% of UV.

  6. Commercial and storefront

    Offices, shops and storefronts. Window film keeps the front of the store from cooking, cuts glare on screens and adds privacy for back offices.

Questions people ask before they book.

How dark can I tint my car windows in Utah?

Front side windows have to let in more than 43% of light. Back side windows and the rear window can go as dark as you want, as long as you have mirrors on both sides. The windshield can only be tinted along the top 4 inches or down to the AS-1 line printed on the glass.

What film do you use?

Geoshield C2 ceramic on vehicles. Patterns are cut on a computer plotter for your exact year, make and model, so nobody is running a blade across your glass.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Mobile tint is available around Cedar City and Southern Utah, for vehicles, equipment and homes. You can also bring it to the shop at 690 N 5400 W.

Can you tint my side-by-side or my excavator?

Yes. RZRs, Rangers, Generals, Xpeditions, Mavericks and Defenders, plus the cab glass on excavators, dozers, skid steers and tractors.

Do you do homes, businesses and campers?

We do. Residential film, commercial and storefront film, and camper and RV windows.
